Business Travel and Expense Guidelines

Document ID: FIN-PROC-004 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 15 May 2026
Company: BluePeak Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (fictional training company)

1. Purpose
These guidelines define approval, booking and reimbursement rules for business travel and reasonable business expenses.

2. Travel approval
Employees must obtain written manager approval before booking travel. Domestic travel should normally be requested at least five working days before departure. International travel requires department-head approval and Finance review. A travel request must state the business purpose, destination, dates, project or cost centre, and estimated cost.

3. Booking classes and limits
Domestic flights under four hours: economy class.
Domestic flights of four hours or more: economy class; premium economy requires director approval.
International flights: economy class unless an executive-level exception is approved.
Hotels: standard business accommodation up to INR 6,000 per night in Tier-1 cities and INR 4,500 per night in other cities, excluding applicable taxes. A higher limit requires prior written approval.
Local transport: use app-based cabs or public transport where practical. Luxury vehicles are not reimbursable without director approval.

4. Meals and incidental expenses
Meal reimbursement is limited to INR 800 per day for domestic travel. Alcohol, tobacco, personal entertainment and expenses for family members are not reimbursable. Client meals require the names of attendees, business purpose and itemized receipt.

5. Expense claims
Submit claims through the Finance Portal within ten calendar days after completing travel. Attach itemized receipts for each expense above INR 300. Claims must include the approved travel request ID, date, category, amount and business purpose. Lost receipts require a signed declaration and Finance approval.

6. Approval matrix
Up to INR 10,000 total claim: reporting manager.
INR 10,001 to INR 50,000: department head.
Above INR 50,000: department head and Finance Controller.
Any exception to a policy limit requires approval before the expense is incurred.

7. Reimbursement timeline
Finance reviews complete claims within five working days. Approved claims are paid in the next scheduled reimbursement cycle, normally within ten working days after approval. Incomplete claims are returned to the employee with comments.

8. Examples of non-reimbursable expenses
Traffic fines, personal shopping, minibar purchases, duplicate claims, commuting between home and the normal office, and costs without a clear business purpose.
