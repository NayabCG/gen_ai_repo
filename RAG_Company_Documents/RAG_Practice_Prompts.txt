RAG PRACTICE PROMPTS — BLUEPEAK TECHNOLOGIES
All documents describe a fictional company and are intended for classroom exercises.
Use the HR policy document already provided as an additional source.

BASIC RETRIEVAL
1. What is the maximum idle screen-lock timeout for company laptops? Cite the document and section.
2. How many working days before joining should the manager submit a role-based access request?
3. What is the daily domestic meal reimbursement limit?
4. Who must approve a production release?
5. How many quotations are required for a purchase above INR 1,00,000?

MULTI-DOCUMENT QUESTIONS
6. An employee is joining next Monday. List the key responsibilities of HR, IT and the hiring manager before and on Day 1.
7. An employee travels domestically, books a hotel in a Tier-1 city for INR 6,800 per night, and submits the claim 12 days after returning. Identify each policy issue and the required approval or action.
8. A developer wants to upload a customer spreadsheet to a personal cloud drive to test a feature. Explain whether this is allowed and what safer approach the policy supports.
9. A vendor will process customer information. What must happen before the vendor receives system or data access?
10. A production release causes a critical user journey to fail. What should the team do, who should be notified, and what follow-up is required?

SCENARIO / REASONING
11. A staff member receives a suspicious MFA approval request and also notices an unfamiliar login. What immediate steps are required?
12. A team wants to buy software costing INR 78,000. Describe the quotation and approval process, and explain what must happen before the vendor starts work.
13. A developer commits an API key to a feature branch. Identify the policy breach and the appropriate security response. Do not invent remediation steps not stated in the documents.
14. A claim is INR 52,000 and contains a missing receipt for an expense of INR 900. Who approves the claim, and what additional evidence is needed?
15. A hotfix is needed urgently outside the normal release process. What approvals, tests and post-release documentation are required?

RAG QUALITY / GROUNDEDNESS TESTS
16. Which policy specifies the exact password length? Answer with a citation.
17. Do the documents state how many annual casual leaves an employee receives? If not, say that the provided documents do not contain this information.
18. Compare the approval requirements for a purchase of INR 20,000, INR 75,000 and INR 1,50,000.
19. Find all deadlines expressed in hours, calendar days or working days across the documents.
20. Answer this question using only the supplied documents: Can employees use an unapproved public generative-AI website with confidential customer data? Quote the relevant rule briefly and cite the source.
