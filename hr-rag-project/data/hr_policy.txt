
ABC Technologies - HR Policy Document

1. Casual Leave Policy

All employees are eligible for 12 casual leaves per calendar year.
Employees can take a maximum of 3 consecutive casual leaves.
Casual leave must be approved by the reporting manager.
Unused casual leaves cannot be carried forward to the next year.

2. Sick Leave Policy

All employees are eligible for 12 sick leaves per year.
Employees taking more than 2 consecutive sick leaves must submit a medical certificate.
Sick leaves cannot be converted into cash.

3. Work From Home Policy

Employees can work from home for a maximum of 2 days per week.
Work from home requires prior approval from the reporting manager.
Employees must be available during official working hours.

4. Maternity Leave Policy

Eligible female employees can take 26 weeks of maternity leave.
Employees must inform HR before applying for maternity leave.
Additional leave may be considered based on company policy.

5. Working Hours Policy

Official working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
Employees must complete 8 working hours per day.
Employees are expected to maintain regular attendance.

6. Salary Policy

Employee salaries are credited on the last working day of every month.
Salary slips are available through the employee HR portal.
Salary-related queries should be raised with the payroll department.

7. Weekend Working Policy

Employees may be requested to work on weekends based on project requirements.
Prior approval from the project manager is required.
Compensatory leave may be provided according to company policy.
