ABC Technologies - HR Policy

1. Casual Leave

Employees are eligible for 12 casual
leaves per calendar year.

All casual leave requests require
manager approval.

2. Sick Leave

Employees are eligible for 10 sick
leaves per calendar year.

A medical certificate may be required
for more than three consecutive days.

3. Work From Home

Employees can request work from home
for up to two days per week.

Manager approval is required.

4. Maternity Leave

Eligible employees are entitled to
26 weeks of maternity leave, subject
to applicable law and eligibility.
