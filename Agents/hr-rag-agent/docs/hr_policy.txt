ABC Technologies – HR Policy

1. Casual Leave Policy

Employees are eligible for 12 casual leaves per calendar year. Casual leave should be requested in advance and approved by the reporting manager.

2. Sick Leave Policy

Employees are eligible for 10 sick leaves per calendar year. A medical certificate may be required for sick leave exceeding three consecutive working days.

3. Work From Home Policy

Employees can request work from home for up to two days per week, subject to manager approval and business requirements.

4. Maternity Leave Policy

Eligible female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of maternity leave, subject to applicable Indian law and eligibility requirements.

5. Notice Period Policy

Employees are required to serve a notice period of 60 days when resigning from the organization, subject to their employment agreement.
